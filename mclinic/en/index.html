A boutique clinic

A place where every person feels well and truly looked after

mClinic grew out of the need to create a friendly, beautiful place — a clinic with time for conversation, where decisions are made without haste. You will find true enthusiasts of healthy, beautiful skin here.

Experienced doctors of dermatology, aesthetic medicine and surgery work side by side with certified cosmetologists — so we look at skin as a whole: from health to appearance.

About the clinic

#SlowMedicine

We never rush the understanding of a patient's needs. An in-depth consultation, a carefully chosen therapy, the highest quality of treatment — in that order.

01 Time

A visit lasts as long as the patient needs — not the schedule. Haste is a poor advisor in medicine.

02 Attention

Before we propose anything, we listen. An in-depth consultation begins every therapy.

03 Diagnosis

First understand the skin — dermatoscopically too — then treat it. Never the other way round.

04 Selection

Treatment protocols and home care are tailor-made. What helped someone else may not be right for you.

05 Restraint

We work for natural, subtle results. Sometimes the best recommendation is less, not more.

06 Continuity

Skin lives in a rhythm of months, not single visits. We plan long-term and stay in touch.

The place

In quiet Wilanów

The clinic sits on Zaściankowa street in Zawady — away from the city noise, with its own car park and an unhurried rhythm of visits. It is part of our philosophy: good decisions about skin are made in calm.

The interiors were designed to feel more like a home than an outpatient unit — bright daylight, natural materials, privacy.
