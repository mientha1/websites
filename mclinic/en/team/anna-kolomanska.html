Cosmetologists

Anna Kołomańska

Cosmetologist

A graduate of the Jagiellonian University Collegium Medicum in Kraków and the College of Engineering and Health in Warsaw. Working by the #SlowMedicine principle, I always look at each patient's overall health — physical condition and mental wellbeing alike. I specialize in therapies for problem skin: acne-prone and vascular. I work both on age prevention and on the visible signs of aging typical of mature skin — always holistically, in the spirit of Healthy Aging.

I keep broadening my knowledge at national congresses and courses in medical and aesthetic cosmetology, at hi-tech cosmetology training, and at courses on gut microflora and its influence on skin conditions. I always start a treatment plan by identifying the cause of the problem, and I choose the right professional home care to support the results of in-clinic work. My work is my passion, and my patients' satisfaction is always the greatest reward.

Treatments performed

HydraFacial® HydraFacial® combines deep cleansing, hydration and skin rejuvenation in one three-step treatment. → AquaPure skin cleansing AquaPure combines hydropeeling, needle-free mesotherapy and a lifting massage, leaving the skin clean, smooth and radiant. → Chemical peels Chemical peels exfoliate the outer layers of the skin and stimulate its natural regeneration — biorevitalisation without needles. → Dermapen 4® microneedling Dermapen 4® fractional microneedling stimulates collagen synthesis, improving skin density and softening scars, stretch marks and wrinkles. → Forlle'd care treatment A care treatment based on Japanese Forlle'd cosmeceuticals, whose low-molecular hyaluronic acid hydrates and rebuilds the skin from within. → Biologique Recherche treatment Biologique Recherche care treatments — from pigmentation reduction to the brand's signature wrinkle-filling massage and intensive caviar rejuvenation. → Cosmelan depigmentation therapy Cosmelan is a depigmentation programme that regulates melanin overproduction and gradually lightens discolouration. → DermaOXY oxygen infusion DermaOXY oxygen infusion drives serum ingredients into the skin with pure oxygen — hydrating, brightening and softening fine lines. → Icoone® Icoone® vacuum massage combines ROBODERM® technology, cold laser and LED light to contour the body and reduce cellulite, swelling and scars. → Maximus Trilipo Maximus Trilipo combines tripolar radiofrequency with muscle stimulation, reducing cellulite and fat while firming skin and strengthening muscles. → Storz acoustic wave therapy Storz Medical acoustic wave therapy non-invasively reduces cellulite and localised fat while improving skin firmness. →
