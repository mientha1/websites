Medical specialists

dr Ewa Piotrowska-Podsiadło

Aesthetic medicine physician, undergoing specialist training in dermatology and venereology

A graduate of the Second Faculty of Medicine at the Medical University of Lublin and of the Postgraduate School of Aesthetic Medicine of the Polish Medical Society in Warsaw. A member of the Polish Dermatological Society and the Polish Society for Aesthetic and Anti-Aging Medicine. She is undergoing specialist training in dermatology and venereology. Her main interests are clinical dermatology and laser therapy, and she has completed numerous courses on the use of botulinum toxin, fillers and autologous preparations.

She diagnoses and treats acute and chronic diseases of the skin, hair and nails. She performs laser procedures — photorejuvenation, laser scar correction, removal of warts and fibromas — as well as dermatosurgery and aesthetic medicine procedures: volumetry, lip augmentation with hyaluronic acid, botulinum toxin and tissue stimulators.

Treatments performed

Botulinum toxin Botulinum toxin relaxes the facial muscles and smooths expression lines — the first effect appears after 5–7 days and lasts 3–6 months. → Hyaluronic acid Hyaluronic acid replenishes a substance naturally present in the skin — filling wrinkles and folds and improving hydration. → Lip enhancement Lip enhancement with hyaluronic acid hydrates the lips, improves proportions, corrects asymmetry and defines the contour. → Facial volumetry Volumetry restores lost facial volume with hyaluronic acid or calcium hydroxyapatite — lifting the cheeks and improving the facial contour. → Profhilo Profhilo strengthens and densifies the skin with a high concentration of thermally stabilised, non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid. → Restylane Skinbooster Restylane Skinbooster delivers stabilised hyaluronic acid into the deeper layers of the skin, activating fibroblasts to produce collagen. → Sunekos 200 and 1200 Sunekos combines non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid with a patented set of amino acids to stimulate skin regeneration — including around the eyes. → Linerase Linerase is a collagen tissue stimulator injected intradermally to activate the skin's own rebuilding processes. → Plinest Fast Plinest Fast is a polynucleotide preparation that stimulates fibroblasts to intensively rebuild young, thin skin. → Sculptra Sculptra stimulates collagen and elastin synthesis with poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA) — the effect builds gradually and can last up to two years. → Nucleofill Nucleofill activates the skin's regenerative mechanisms at the cellular level, stimulating the production of collagen, elastin and hyaluronic acid. → Consultation Every treatment at mClinic — aesthetic medicine, dermatology or cosmetology — begins with a consultation and a detailed medical history. → Dermoscopy Dermoscopy is a non-invasive, magnified examination of moles that allows early detection of suspicious skin lesions, including melanoma. → Dermatologic surgery Dermatologic surgery removes skin lesions with a scalpel or CO2 laser, under local anaesthesia, with histopathology when needed. → BBL® HERO photorejuvenation BBL® HERO broadband light evens skin tone, reduces pigmentation, vessels and redness, calms acne and stimulates collagen. → BBL Clear V Clear V® selectively closes capillaries and reduces vascular lesions on the face and body without damaging the epidermis. → BBL Clear Silk Clear Silk is a gentle non-ablative laser treatment that reduces redness, acne and acne scars while smoothing skin texture. → Dye-VL pigmentation and redness treatment Dye-VL narrowband light reduces pigmentation and redness, with results usually visible immediately. → Lavieen thulium laser The Lavieen thulium laser reduces pigmentation, scars and stretch marks and smooths wrinkles, working in ablative, sub-ablative and non-ablative modes. → Fractional CO2 laser The fractional CO2 laser rebuilds skin through controlled micro-injuries, reducing wrinkles, scars, stretch marks and pigmentation while tightening the skin. → Erbium-glass laser The non-ablative 1540 nm erbium-glass laser remodels the dermis without disturbing the epidermis, improving skin tension, tone and texture. → Secret RF DUO microneedle radiofrequency Secret RF DUO combines microneedle radiofrequency with a 1540 nm fractional erbium-glass laser to tighten skin and reduce wrinkles, scars and stretch marks. →
