What we work on — and why

Devices do not treat — people do. But good instruments allow us to work more precisely, more safely and with predictable results. These are the systems we work on, and why we chose them.

BroadBand Light (420–1400 nm) with smart filters in a single handpiece.One system, many protocol-backed applications: Forever Young photorejuvenation, Forever Clear acne therapy, vascular clearance, pigment reduction, SkinTyte.

Used forpigmentation · capillaries & erythema · acne · photorejuvenation
