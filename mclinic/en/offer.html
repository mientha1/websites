Treatments

The mClinic offer

Five fields, one principle: diagnosis first, then the choice of treatment. You can also start from your concern — we will guide you to the right methods.

The foundation

Dermatology

Dermatology is the foundation of mClinic. Before we propose anything, we want to understand the skin — its history, condition and needs.

Consultation Every treatment at mClinic — aesthetic medicine, dermatology or cosmetology — begins with a consultation and a detailed medical history. od 150 zł→ Dermoscopy Dermoscopy is a non-invasive, magnified examination of moles that allows early detection of suspicious skin lesions, including melanoma. od 350 zł→ Dermatologic surgery Dermatologic surgery removes skin lesions with a scalpel or CO2 laser, under local anaesthesia, with histopathology when needed. od 450 zł→

Restraint and proportion

Aesthetic medicine

We see aesthetic medicine as an extension of dermatology: healthy skin first, then its appearance. We work for natural, subtle results — never to change the character of a face.

Consultation Every treatment at mClinic — aesthetic medicine, dermatology or cosmetology — begins with a consultation and a detailed medical history. od 150 zł→ Botulinum toxin Botulinum toxin relaxes the facial muscles and smooths expression lines — the first effect appears after 5–7 days and lasts 3–6 months. od 500 zł→ Hyaluronic acid Hyaluronic acid replenishes a substance naturally present in the skin — filling wrinkles and folds and improving hydration. od 1200 zł→ Lip enhancement Lip enhancement with hyaluronic acid hydrates the lips, improves proportions, corrects asymmetry and defines the contour. od 1300 zł→ Facial volumetry Volumetry restores lost facial volume with hyaluronic acid or calcium hydroxyapatite — lifting the cheeks and improving the facial contour. 2400 zł→

Technology as an instrument

Laser & hi-tech

Light and energy let us work precisely where creams cannot reach: pigmentation, capillaries, scars, loss of firmness. Technology here is an instrument — not a promise.

BBL® HERO photorejuvenation BBL® HERO broadband light evens skin tone, reduces pigmentation, vessels and redness, calms acne and stimulates collagen. od 1200 zł→ BBL Clear V Clear V® selectively closes capillaries and reduces vascular lesions on the face and body without damaging the epidermis. od 400 zł→ BBL Clear Silk Clear Silk is a gentle non-ablative laser treatment that reduces redness, acne and acne scars while smoothing skin texture. od 1500 zł→ Lavieen thulium laser The Lavieen thulium laser reduces pigmentation, scars and stretch marks and smooths wrinkles, working in ablative, sub-ablative and non-ablative modes. od 1200 zł→ Secret RF DUO microneedle radiofrequency Secret RF DUO combines microneedle radiofrequency with a 1540 nm fractional erbium-glass laser to tighten skin and reduce wrinkles, scars and stretch marks. od 1500 zł→

Surgical precision

Surgery

The surgical scope of mClinic is led by a specialist in general surgery. Procedures are performed on an outpatient basis, with full preparation and aftercare.

Blepharoplasty Blepharoplasty surgically corrects the eyelids, removing excess skin and fatty tissue to improve both vision comfort and the look of the eye area. od 6500 zł→

Care and continuity

Cosmetology

Cosmetology at mClinic works hand in hand with the medical practice: preparing skin for procedures, maintaining their results and guiding daily care at a clinical level.
