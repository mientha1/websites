Your concern

Enlarged pores

Pores serve a natural excretory function and cannot be removed entirely. They can, however, be narrowed and made much less visible.

Understanding the concern

Everyone has pores — they release sweat, sebum and metabolic by-products. Excessively enlarged pores signal overactive sebaceous glands, leading to excess sebum, shine and blackhead-prone skin. They appear across the whole face or in the T-zone and mainly affect combination, oily and acne-prone skin.

Enlarged pores are among the harder skin imperfections to improve: the problem is genetically and hormonally driven, and skin tends to revert after single treatments. It is a chronic, recurring concern that responds best to a series of in-clinic procedures combined with sebum-regulating home care.

Possible approaches

These are possible directions, not a prescription. The choice of method always follows a consultation and examination.

Fractional CO2 laser Fractional CO2 laser resurfacing remodels the skin, reducing enlarged pores and seborrhea. → Secret RF DUO microneedle radiofrequency Secret RF microneedle radiofrequency shrinks pore visibility and improves skin texture. → Dermapen 4® microneedling Dermapen 4 microneedling stimulates collagen synthesis and reduces pore visibility. → HydraFacial® HydraFacial deeply cleanses the skin; its sebum-regulating serum suits oily, problem-prone complexions. → Chemical peels Chemical peels exfoliate the skin, tighten pores and curb sebum production. → BBL® HERO photorejuvenation BBL phototherapy improves skin firmness and texture, supporting imperfection reduction. → Icoone® Icoone stimulates the skin across its surface, improving quality and firmness. →
