Cosmetology

Forlle'd care treatment

A care treatment based on Japanese Forlle'd cosmeceuticals, whose low-molecular hyaluronic acid hydrates and rebuilds the skin from within.

Forlle'd is a Japanese cosmeceutical brand with clinically proven formulas. Its technology is based on low-molecular hyaluronic acid with ionised minerals — 5 nm particles that penetrate deeply and hydrate the skin from within, rebuilding its structure, stimulating cell regeneration, detoxifying and strengthening the skin's resilience against irritants.

Treatments are tailored to your skin's needs. mClinic offers three rituals — hydrating, platinum and lifting — each lasting about an hour and covering the face, neck and hands.

Who is it for

For first signs of ageing, mature skin, dry skin, support with pigmentation, and to prepare the skin for aesthetic-medicine procedures or care for it afterwards.
