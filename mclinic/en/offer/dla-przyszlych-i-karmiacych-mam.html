Care

For mothers-to-be & breastfeeding mothers

Pregnancy and breastfeeding change skin and body — and most procedures are not advised then. We have selected those that are safe, and we carry them out with particular care.

You do not have to give up looking after yourself during this time. Treatments are chosen individually, always after a conversation about your stage of pregnancy or breastfeeding.
