Mesotherapy

Needle mesotherapy delivers revitalising and vitamin preparations into the dermis — deeply hydrating, nourishing and firming the skin.

Needle mesotherapy is a minimally invasive treatment in which revitalising, vitamin or medicinal preparations are delivered into the dermis through fine, densely placed micro-punctures. It deeply hydrates, nourishes and revitalises the skin, improving firmness and elasticity by stimulating fibroblasts to produce new collagen.

Active ingredients include hyaluronic acid, vitamins, amino acids, microelements and biomimetic peptides. The preparation is matched to your individual skin type.

Who is it for

For dry, dull, greyish skin and the first signs of ageing — fine wrinkles and loss of elasticity. Most often performed on the face, eye area, neck, décolletage and backs of the hands; also used for hair loss and stretch marks.

Good to know

Performed painlessly under local anaesthesia; a session takes 30–40 minutes with no downtime. Administered in series at 2–4 week intervals.
