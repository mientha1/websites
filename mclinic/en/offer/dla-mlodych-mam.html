Care

For new mothers

After childbirth, skin and body need time — and sensible support. We help you recover on your own terms, without pressure.

The most common topics are stretch marks, skin laxity, cellulite and post-pregnancy skin changes. The plan is built during a consultation, around your calendar and how you feel.
