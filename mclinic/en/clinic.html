The clinic

A singular place

mClinic is a boutique clinic in quiet Wilanów — a friendly, beautiful place where every person is meant to feel well and truly looked after.

A clinic with a mission — the founder's letter

mClinic grew from a vision I had carried in my head for a long time. I had always wanted to create a friendly, beautiful place where every patient would feel welcomed, encouraged, safe and well cared for.

In my professional life I have worked in large clinics and small practices alike — and those experiences helped me create a quality of my own. Organising the clinic, I chose both the equipment and the people with great care: experienced dermatologists and cosmetology specialists. True enthusiasts of healthy, beautiful skin.

It matters deeply to me to be surrounded by good energy and good people — they are the heart of this place. Thanks to the collaboration of experienced experts and modern, proven, safe technologies, we can approach skin holistically — in terms of health and beauty.

At mClinic we focus on the individual needs of each patient and create tailor-made treatment protocols as well as home care.

A warm welcome to all of you — Magdalena Pisula, MD

#SlowMedicine

In the spirit of #SlowMedicine, we never rush the understanding of a patient's needs.

01 Time

A visit lasts as long as the patient needs — not the schedule. Haste is a poor advisor in medicine.

02 Attention

Before we propose anything, we listen. An in-depth consultation begins every therapy.

03 Diagnosis

First understand the skin — dermatoscopically too — then treat it. Never the other way round.

04 Selection

Treatment protocols and home care are tailor-made. What helped someone else may not be right for you.

05 Restraint

We work for natural, subtle results. Sometimes the best recommendation is less, not more.

06 Continuity

Skin lives in a rhythm of months, not single visits. We plan long-term and stay in touch.
