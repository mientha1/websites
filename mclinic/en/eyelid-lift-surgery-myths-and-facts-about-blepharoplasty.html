Eyelid Lift Surgery - Myths and Facts about Blepharoplasty

August 22, 2023 mClinic

Drooping eyelids are a problem that not only affects appearance but can also negatively impact self-esteem and limit our field of vision. At mClinic, in collaboration with Dr. Kalina Jędrzejko, MD, we propose treatment methods that effectively solve this problem. In this post, we will debunk the five most common myths about drooping eyelids.

Myth 1: Blepharoplasty creates an unnatural appearance

At mClinic, we approach the problem of drooping eyelids with great care, understanding how it affects the appearance and well-being of our patients. Therefore, we focus on modernity and precision in every procedure. Blepharoplasty performed in our clinic has nothing to do with outdated surgical methods. Under the experienced eye of Dr. Kalina Jędrzejko, MD, this procedure becomes a true art, allowing for the correction of both skin and muscles without the risk of unnatural effects. We analyze each case individually, adapting to the unique needs of the patient. At mClinic, you can be sure that your appearance is in good hands, and the results will be fully natural and in line with your expectations.

Myth 2: Surgery is the only option for treating drooping eyelids

Depending on the degree of severity of the problem, other methods such as laser therapy or botox are also available. These options can effectively solve the problem without the need for surgery.

Myth 3: Drooping eyelids are only an aesthetic inconvenience

Improperly assessed as merely an aesthetic problem, drooping eyelids can indeed lead to a restricted field of vision and even a deterioration in eyesight. At mClinic, we always strive not only to improve appearance but also to enhance the quality of life and health of our patients.

Myth 4: Only older people have problems with drooping eyelids

Although the natural aging process can contribute to drooping eyelids, younger people may also experience this problem. Factors such as genetics or sun exposure play a key role here.

Myth 5: Skillful makeup will solve the problem of drooping eyelids

While makeup can help mask the problem at an early stage, as the situation deepens with age, cosmetics cease to be an effective solution.

Drooping eyelids don't have to be a source of concern and frustration. At mClinic, in collaboration with experienced surgeon Dr. Kalina Jędrzejko, MD, we offer various treatment methods tailored to the individual needs of each patient. If you would like to learn more, please contact us and schedule a consultation at our clinic in Wilanów.
