Business

A private place to talk

Important decisions have always been made in particular places. Pałac Pass operates as a private conference and business centre — near Warsaw, and yet entirely apart.

Meetings

Reputation is built by setting

The rooms of a genuine residence lend a meeting a standing no hotel conference floor can. Pass hosts small-group trainings, symposia, conferences, banquets, and team retreats — including weekend stays.

The spacious, air-conditioned rooms favour concentration, and the historic setting excludes nothing modern: period elegance and present-day technology hold a coherent dialogue here.

In the open

Negotiations in the shade of old trees

The park, with its quiet ponds and great trees, is a natural setting for discreet talks — and a long walk often achieves more than another hour at the table.

After a working day, guests have the run of the relaxation suite with sauna and jacuzzi: a moment to recover before the next day of meetings.
