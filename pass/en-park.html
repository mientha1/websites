The Park

An English garden between two rivers

Seventeen hectares of historic landscape park at the fork of the Utrata and Rokitnica — an enclave of silence where mostly birdsong is heard.

Landscape

An outstanding work of garden art

The park at Pass belongs to the so-called Kampinos group — the family of historic gardens of western Mazovia — and in its variety surpasses them all, including those of Pęcice and Leszno. Atmospheric ponds, majestic trees and open meadows compose the kind of landscape English garden art perfected: natural in appearance, considered in every plane.

White gazebos stand among the greenery, and at the heart of the palace garden — a black orangery of more than 375 m², where garden receptions are held.

Stewardship

A patient return to glory

The park’s restoration is work measured in years, not seasons — carried out with the support of the environmental office of the town of Błonie, which helps care for the listed monuments of nature. Rare birds gather at the ponds; walks through the park and along the water belong to every visit at Pass.
