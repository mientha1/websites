Weddings

A reception at the residence

Good stories begin in places remembered for years. The palace interiors seat up to 60 guests; the black orangery in the garden, up to 170.

Interiors

A ball in period rooms

Marble floors, crystal chandeliers, mirrors and antique furniture turn a reception into a ball — no scenography required, because the elegance is built into the architecture. The rooms take styling well, from glamour through modern to vintage.

For all the formality of the state rooms, guests feel at home here. Pass is a residence, not a banqueting hall — a difference you see in the photographs and hear in the conversations long after the wedding.

Garden & orangery

The tradition of English gardens

Seventeen hectares of park, with its ponds and white gazebos, make a setting that photographs beautifully in every season. At the heart of the garden stands the black orangery: over 375 m² of floor, and room for 170 guests.

The garden welcomes freer styling than the interiors — garden parties, rustic tables, boho. In the evening, the lights of the orangery and the dark green of the park do the rest.

History lends the place its weight: Chopin was born not far from here, and in nearby Błonie Napoleon first met Maria Walewska. Good company for important vows.
